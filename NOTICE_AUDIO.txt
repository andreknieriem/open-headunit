Third-party audio software in Open Headunit
==========================================

These components are compiled into libhur_time_stretch.so and are included in
this APK even when the OHU time-correction algorithm is selected.

OHU uses only the pinned R3 implementation, its built-in FFT and BQ resampler.
The local 2026-10-24 change in src/finer/PhaseAdvance.h skips peak-map and
strongest-channel preparation only when the input and output hops are equal;
phase/history updates are retained. The adapter/build subset and this change
are described in app/src/main/cpp/third_party/README.md, included below.
